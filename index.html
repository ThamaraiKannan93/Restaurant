Our Story

Rooted in the seasons, guided by the flame

Saffron & Sage began with a simple belief — that the best meals are honest ones. We work with local farms and fishers, letting each ingredient lead. Our wood-fired hearth is the heart of the kitchen, coaxing depth and smoke from everything it touches.

The result is food that feels both refined and unfussy: familiar flavours, reimagined with care and a little theatre.
